# Hospital Management Analysis Dashboard

An Excel dashboard built with pivot tables, KPI cards, charts and slicers.

## Dashboard Preview

<img src="Hospital_Dash.png" width="700">

## Objective

To analyze hospital data and understand patient, department and billing performance.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Charts
- Slicers
- KPI Cards

## Project Files

- Hospital_Dashboard.xlsx: final dashboard (download to view)
- Dataset: raw data

## Key KPIs

- Total admissions: 1,000
- Revenue: ₹5.06 Cr (treatment + medication)
- Average length of stay: 4.4 days
- Average patient satisfaction: 3.79 / 5
- Emergency admissions: 37.6% of all admissions
- Readmission rate: 8.1%

## Key Insights

- General Medicine is the busiest department, with the highest treatment cost, followed by Cardiology.
- About 37.6% of admissions are emergencies, so emergency beds and staff must always be ready.
- Viral Fever and Dengue are the most common diagnoses, which points to a seasonal infection load.
- About 70% of bills are fully paid, but around 30% are partially paid or pending, which is a revenue collection risk.
- Monthly revenue stays steady at roughly 35 to 50 lakh, with a peak in April.
- 87% of patients recovered, 8% were readmitted and 5% were referred, which shows good treatment outcomes.
- The satisfaction score of 3.79 out of 5 is decent but has room to improve.

## Recommendations

- Add staff and beds in General Medicine and Emergency.
- Follow up on pending and partially paid bills.
- Prepare for infection cases (fever and dengue) in the high season.
- Reduce readmissions with better follow-up care.
